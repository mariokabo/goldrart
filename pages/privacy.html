Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 18, 2026

1. Introduction

GoldrArt ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://goldrart.com (the "Site").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Contact Information: When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Payment Information: We collect payment method preferences (not actual card numbers, which are handled securely).
  • Order Details: Information about products you view, add to cart, or purchase.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser and Device Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, interactions with content, and search queries.
  • Cookies and Tracking: We use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze site performance.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking (governed by Google's privacy policies).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing customer support
  • Improving our website, products, and services
  • Conducting marketing and promotional activities (with your consent)
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring website security

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share your information with:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: Delivery partners, payment processors, and communication platforms (WhatsApp).
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Google Services: Google Analytics and Google Ads for performance tracking and advertising.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) to prevent unauthorized scripts
  • Regular security audits and updates

Note: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You assume some risk when providing information online.

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the information provided below.
